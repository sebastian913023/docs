Method and validation status

Probability. Random-walk model: P(up) = Phi(z), where z is the log distance from the window-open reference divided by minute volatility times the square root of minutes left. Volatility is an exponentially weighted estimate over 60 one-minute returns.

Decision. A signal needs a decision minute between 3 and 10, fresh data, and edge after the Kalshi taker fee at or above your minimum. Stake is fractional Kelly with a hard cap.

What has been tested. The Python engine in this repo passed synthetic tests: on a pure random walk it reported no significant edge, and with a planted drift it detected the edge. The full engine adds a walk-forward logistic layer that this page does not run.

What has not. Nothing here is validated on real Kalshi history or real Kalshi prices. A signal is a model estimate, not a prediction of the outcome, and no model wins every window.

  • Spot candles come from Twelve Data through your connector, as a proxy for the CF Benchmarks index Kalshi settles on.
  • Window references use the last closed 1-minute bar, not the 60-second index average.
  • Fee formula, series coverage and tie rules need checking against Kalshi's current rulebook.
  • Run the backtest on your own data before sizing real money.
